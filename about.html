Our Story

Fashion as a Form of Self-Expression

Dollar was born from a simple belief — that what you wear is a statement before you say a word. We are a visionary fashion label rooted in South Africa, designing pieces that feel as good as they look.

Every stitch, every silhouette, every colour is chosen with intention. We don't follow trends — we create a wardrobe built to last, styled to be unmistakably you.

Our collections blend timeless tailoring with a modern sensibility, made accessible to everyone.
